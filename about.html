Plus Responder

More Than a Service. A Lifeline When It Matters Most.

At Plus Responder, we believe that access to healthcare should never feel complicated, especially when someone needs it the most. Whether a family requires an ambulance during an emergency or a loved one needs professional medical assistance at home, finding the right support at the right time can make all the difference.

We are building a convenient healthcare access platform that connects people with ambulance booking services and qualified healthcare professionals for home-based care. Our goal is to simplify the journey from searching for medical assistance to connecting with the right service, helping individuals and families navigate healthcare with greater confidence and peace of mind.

Healthcare That Comes Closer to You

Healthcare should adapt to people's needs, not the other way around. We embrace a more accessible and patient-focused approach by bringing essential healthcare connections closer to homes, families and communities.

We aim to reduce the effort involved in finding medical transportation and home healthcare support through a convenient, technology-enabled experience. By connecting patients with relevant service providers, we strive to make healthcare access more organised, approachable and convenient.

Our Purpose

Making Every Connection Count

Behind every booking is a person, a family, and a need that matters. We recognise that healthcare decisions often come with urgency, uncertainty, and emotional stress.

That is why our purpose goes beyond facilitating bookings. We aim to create a dependable bridge between people seeking medical assistance and the professionals who provide it. Every interaction is an opportunity to make accessing care simpler and to help families feel better supported during important moments.

Our Mission

To simplify access to essential healthcare services by connecting individuals and families with ambulance providers and qualified healthcare professionals, making medical assistance more accessible, convenient, and patient-focused.

Our Vision

To become a trusted healthcare access platform that transforms how people connect with medical transportation and home healthcare services, building a more connected and responsive healthcare ecosystem for communities across India.

Our Promise

Our Promise to Every Family

At Plus Responder, we understand that healthcare is deeply personal. Behind every request is someone looking for reassurance, support, and a reliable way forward.

We are committed to making the process of finding healthcare assistance simpler and more convenient. Through continuous improvement, responsible service connections, and a patient-first mindset, we aim to make a meaningful difference in how individuals and families access essential care.

Because when healthcare becomes easier to access, families can focus on what matters most: the well-being of the people they love.
