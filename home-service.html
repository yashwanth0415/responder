Our Services

Explore Our Home Healthcare Services

1

Doctor at Home

Accessing medical advice can be challenging for patients with limited mobility or those who find travelling difficult. Home doctor visits, where available, can provide medical assessments, follow-up consultations, and professional guidance in a familiar environment. Home consultations may be suitable for senior citizens, patients recovering from illness, and individuals who require a medical review but do not need emergency hospital treatment.

2

Nursing Services at Home

Professional nursing assistance can help patients manage their care requirements outside a hospital setting. Depending on the patient's needs, home nursing may include vital-sign monitoring, wound dressing, prescribed medication assistance, injections when clinically appropriate, and post-operative nursing care, subject to professional assessment and provider availability.

3

Patient Care Attendant at Home

When a loved one needs regular assistance, a patient care attendant can help with everyday activities and personal care. Depending on their training and assigned responsibilities, attendants may assist with mobility, bathing, dressing, feeding, repositioning, and hygiene.

4

Elderly Care Services at Home

Senior citizens often benefit from additional support that helps them maintain comfort, dignity, and independence. Home elderly care may include companionship, mobility assistance, personal hygiene, meal assistance, and help with daily routines.

5

Physiotherapy at Home

Recovering mobility and strength can require consistent rehabilitation. Home physiotherapy may provide convenient access to qualified physiotherapists for individualised sessions supporting post-surgical recovery, mobility improvement, balance training, strength development, and rehabilitation. Treatment plans should be determined by the physiotherapist following an assessment.

6

Post-Hospitalisation Care at Home

Returning home after hospitalisation can involve additional care and recovery requirements. Appropriate home healthcare support may help patients follow their prescribed care plans, manage daily activities, and receive nursing assistance where necessary, based on the treating doctor's recommendations.

7

Post-Surgery Care at Home

Recovery after surgery may require assistance with mobility, personal care, prescribed medication routines, wound care by qualified professionals, and follow-up arrangements, subject to the patient's medical needs and the availability of appropriately qualified professionals.

8

Mother and Newborn Care at Home

The period following childbirth is important for both mother and baby. Depending on local availability, families may explore qualified postnatal nursing assistance, newborn care guidance, and practical support during the mother's recovery. Medical concerns should always be assessed by a qualified healthcare professional.

9

Personal Care and Daily Living Assistance

Some individuals need support with daily activities without requiring continuous hospital-based care. Home care assistance may include bathing, dressing, feeding, hygiene, mobility support, and help with maintaining a regular routine, matched to the patient's requirements and the caregiver's training.

Service availability varies by location. Home healthcare is not a substitute for emergency treatment, and patients requiring critical care must receive appropriate medical assessment.

Who Can Benefit

Who Can Benefit from Home Healthcare?

  • Senior citizens who need assistance with everyday activities.
  • Patients recovering after surgery or hospital discharge.
  • Individuals with limited mobility or physical disabilities.
  • Patients requiring prescribed nursing assistance.
  • People undergoing physiotherapy or rehabilitation.
  • Individuals with long-term health conditions who need appropriate ongoing support.
  • Mothers and newborns requiring suitable postnatal assistance.
  • Families seeking additional support with patient care at home.

The suitability of home healthcare depends on the patient's condition, required level of supervision, and the capabilities of the assigned professional.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions About Home Healthcare

What are home healthcare services?

Home healthcare services provide appropriate medical or personal care support to patients in their homes. Depending on the patient's requirements, these may include nursing, doctor visits, physiotherapy, patient attendants, elderly care, and post-hospitalisation support.

Can I book a nurse at home through Plus Responder?

You can enquire about home nursing services through Plus Responder. Availability depends on your location, the patient's care requirements, and the availability of an appropriately qualified nursing professional.

Can I arrange a patient attendant for my parents?

Patient attendants may assist senior citizens with personal care, mobility, hygiene, feeding, and daily activities. Responsibilities depend on the patient's needs and the attendant's training.

Is physiotherapy available at home?

Home physiotherapy may be available through qualified physiotherapists in supported locations. The appropriate treatment plan and frequency should be determined following a professional assessment.

Can I arrange home care after surgery?

Suitable post-operative support may be arranged depending on the patient's condition and local service availability. Follow the treating doctor's recommendations when selecting the required level of care.

Is home healthcare suitable for elderly people?

Home healthcare may help senior citizens with mobility, personal care, daily activities, and other appropriate support requirements. The care arrangement should reflect their individual needs and safety considerations.

Can I book home healthcare for a few hours or a full day?

Scheduling options depend on the service category and provider availability. Specify whether you need a short visit, a scheduled shift, overnight assistance, or extended care when enquiring.

How much do home healthcare services cost?

Pricing depends on the service type, professional qualifications, location, duration, timing, and care requirements. Confirm the applicable charges before booking.

Can home healthcare replace hospital treatment?

No. Home healthcare is appropriate only when the patient's condition can be managed safely at home. Patients who require emergency treatment, hospital admission, or intensive clinical monitoring need appropriate medical care.

How do I find home healthcare services near me?

Share your location and required service with Plus Responder to explore available options. Confirm the provider's qualifications, service scope, schedule, and charges before booking.

Emergency notice: For life-threatening emergencies in India, call 112 or seek immediate emergency medical assistance. Do not delay emergency care while arranging a home healthcare service.
